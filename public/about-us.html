WE ARE AN AWARD WINNING

Motion graphics  and design agency

We started in 2019 as a small group of designers making social videos in After Effects. We've grown a lot since then, but the idea hasn't changed: give brands top-tier animation and design without the cost and hassle of building an in-house team. Today we cover UI design, 2D, and 3D animation, and we help SaaS and tech companies move their brand forward.

500+ videos made every year

Video and animation are the bread and butter of what we do. Most of our clients come to us to bring their ideas to life, whether it’s a 90-second explainer or 10 to 20 short animations each month in different formats and languages. Our services cover everything from training and explainer videos to hero animations, product launches, and brand visuals.

We work on a subscription model, giving businesses a dedicated creative team for all their design needs. Clients can submit unlimited projects, and we help prioritize and deliver each one based on their goals and timelines.

Communication

Effective communication is crucial for building trust with our clients on their animation and design projects. We encourage face-to-face meetings (though Google Meet, Zoom, and Teams calls are more common) and are flexible in adapting to our clients' preferred processes. It’s common for us to set up a dedicated Slack channel for seamless communication, but if another platform works better for you, we are happy to adapt.

Who we are

We’re a small agency, and with that comes the personalized benefits of a close-knit team. You’ll have direct access to our Founder, Sam, and can easily book time with your project manager or the animation team whenever needed. Though we are a UK-based business registered in the City of London, our team operates remotely across the globe, allowing us to cover multiple time zones. This enables us to swiftly handle revisions, draft new concepts, and respond quickly to client needs.

Who we work with

Our clients come from all over the world, with most based in the US, UK, and Europe. We work closely with SaaS companies, tech brands, and software teams, using Figma and the Adobe Suite to keep everything smooth and collaborative. While tech is a big part of what we do, we’ve teamed up with clients from all kinds of industries and company sizes. In 2025, we got to work with early-stage startups and large enterprises managing multiple brands, as well as over half a dozen YC-backed companies preparing visuals for their Demo Day launches. No matter who we work with, every project is tailored to their goals and needs.

Our goals
for the future

Our goal isn't to be the biggest studio. It's to stay excellent at what we do well and keep raising the bar on the work itself.

AI is changing creative production quickly, and we use it where it makes sense. But a lot of AI-generated video looks the same right now, and our clients come to us because they don't want to look like everyone else. The creative execution stays with our team.

We share what we're thinking on our blog and YouTube channel if you want to follow along.
