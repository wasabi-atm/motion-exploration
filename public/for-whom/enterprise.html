Frequently asked questions

Questions
enterprises ask us most often

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

What is enterprise video production?

It's video production built for the way large companies actually consume creative, as an ongoing stream across many campaigns, teams, and markets, rather than as one-off projects. The hard part usually isn't any single video, it's keeping quality and brand consistency steady across all of them, which is why most enterprise clients work with us on a subscription.

How does pricing work for enterprise teams?

Enterprise clients can use our subscription model for ongoing video and animation needs. For example, Obrizum saved over $350,000 across 108 projects by using our subscription, gaining consistent high-quality output while reducing overall production costs.

Are you experienced with SaaS, tech, and global enterprise clients?

Yes. Our core focus is enterprise SaaS, tech, and AI clients. We've worked with global brands like ClickUp, Apollo, Obrizum, GotPhoto, and many more, giving us deep experience with the complexities of enterprise campaigns, including stakeholder alignment, multi-market launches, and brand compliance.

Can you handle multiple projects simultaneously for multiple different campaigns?

Yes. We act as an extension of your internal team and are set up to manage multiple projects across different campaigns at the same time, covering various formats, languages, and styles. For example, with our client Planful, we worked on 4–5 different brandings and brand extensions, not to mention simultaneous event campaigns, all while keeping production efficient and on track.

Why do big corporations like Apollo, ClickUp, and Obrizum choose Motion The Agency?

We're a full-service motion design partner, handling everything from ideation to final animation. For subscription clients, we don't assign just one person. We build a dedicated team of experts across design, animation, and motion graphics, alongside your dedicated account manager and project manager, so a single subscription gets multiple specialists working together to deliver high-quality content at scale.

What types of projects can Motion The Agency handle for enterprise teams?

We handle everything from explainer videos, sizzle reels, and product demos to corporate videos, UI animation, Lottie files, and branded motion graphics. We also support web design, brand identity, and UI/UX design.

What if we need changes after the video is delivered?

We work with you until it's right. If something needs adjusting after delivery, tell us what's off and we'll work through the changes with you rather than pointing at a revision policy.
