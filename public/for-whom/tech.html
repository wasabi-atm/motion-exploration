What we learned

Tech buyers need more than big claims

Your technology might be faster, smarter, or genuinely different. But buyers still need to see the proof. Clear information, real product detail, and a believable demonstration give them something they can understand, trust, and assess for themselves.
82% of tech buyers trust peer recommendations more than what a vendor says about itself. Your video still needs to earn that trust by being clear, specific, and believable.
45% of developers say poor or inaccurate technical information is their biggest barrier to trying something new. A clear explanation can be the difference between curiosity and drop-off.
Developers and engineers have little patience for content that feels vague, exaggerated, or overly sales-led. They want useful information they can assess for themselves.
Buyers who have seen a product working are much more likely to convert than those reached through marketing alone, up to six times more likely in some benchmarks.

Frequently asked questions

Questions tech companies
usually ask us

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

What is tech explainer video production?

It's video content built to explain a technology product, service, or concept clearly, without oversimplifying it to the point a tech viewer would call it inaccurate. It's meant to work for both a first time viewer and someone who actually understands the technology.

How do you make sure the video is technically accurate?

We ask for input from someone on your team who understands the technology deeply, not just marketing materials, and we build the script and visuals around that. Before anything ships, we check it against what you told us rather than assuming we got the concept right on the first pass.

Our product is genuinely complex. Won't simplifying it for video make it inaccurate?

Not if it's done carefully. We separate the parts that need to stay precise from the parts that can be simplified for a first time viewer, so the video is easier to follow without misrepresenting what your technology actually does.

Our audience includes engineers who'll notice if something's wrong. How do you handle that?

We build in a technical review step specifically for this, since a single inaccurate detail can undo the credibility of an otherwise good video with a tech audience. Tell us this matters upfront and we'll make sure someone technical reviews the script before we animate anything.

Can this video work for both investors and tech buyers?

Often yes, though it depends how technical each audience is. We can build a layered script that leads with the plain version and holds the technical detail underneath it, so both audiences get what they need from the same video.

What if we need changes after the video is delivered?

We work with you until it's right. If something needs adjusting after delivery, tell us what's off and we'll work through the changes with you rather than pointing at a revision policy.

Can we use the video across different platforms?

Yes. We deliver your final video in the formats each channel needs, so the same project works on your website, social channels, and anywhere else you plan to use it.

What's the pricing structure, and how do I get started?

We offer a fixed price per video with a clear quote upfront, or a monthly subscription if you're likely to need ongoing content. Book a call or request a free sample and we'll ask a few questions about your technology before quoting.
