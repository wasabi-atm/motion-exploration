Key factors

What actually affects video production cost?

Not all videos are built the same, so the cost can change depending on what you need. To keep things transparent, here are a few key factors that usually affect pricing:

Video length

Longer videos take more time to script, design, animate, and polish.

Animation style

A simple 2D animation will usually cost less than something with complex motion, detailed scenes, or 3D elements.

Revision rounds

More feedback means more iterations, which can add to the overall production time.

Timeline

Need it quickly? A shorter turnaround may require more people working on the project at once, which can affect the cost.
As a guide, our pricing usually starts from around $3,480 for a standard 30-second video
and can go up to around $7,300 for a 150-second enhanced video.
