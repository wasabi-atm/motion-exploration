Challenges Obrizum faced

Increase in video demand with a small internal team

As Obrizum’s client base grew, they faced internal bottlenecks due to rising video production needs and a limited in-house team.

Unpredictable video needs

Most of Obrizum’s videos were created for clients, with demand fluctuating month to month. Hiring a full creative team wasn’t ideal when workloads constantly shifted.

Too high of a cost

With the volume and variety of videos required, working with traditional agencies would have been too costly for Obrizum.
