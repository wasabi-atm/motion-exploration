Frequently asked questions

Got Questions?
We've got answers

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

Which plan should we choose?

Basic suits steady social and marketing content. Premium suits teams running events, training courses or several website videos at once, and it is where most clients land. Custom is for a rebrand, a scale-up or a raise, where the work spans video, design systems and web. Send us a brief and we will tell you which one your volume actually needs.

What does the monthly price include?

Your assigned team, unlimited revisions, same day response and monthly reporting. Basic covers graphic design and simple 2D animation with two specialists. Premium adds UI design, storyboard planning and complex 2D and 3D with three. Custom adds prototyping, design systems and website development.

What is the difference between Basic and Premium?

Team size and what they can make. Basic gives you two specialists working on simpler 2D. Premium gives you three, adds storyboard planning and 3D, and puts your work at high priority so it moves first when several requests land together.

How much work can we send in a month?

You send as much as you need, and the team works the queue. The realistic answer depends on the size of each request, so on a first call we will look at what you are actually shipping and tell you which plan carries it.

How fast will we get work back?

You get a same day response on every request, and your project manager confirms delivery dates as work enters the queue. Premium and Custom sit at high priority.

How does billing work?

Everything is paid upfront and your plan runs on the calendar month. Annual payment is available at a discount.

Can we change or pause our plan?

Yes. Move up when volume grows, down when it settles, and pause when you need to. We work through the details with you on a call rather than holding you to a rule written on a pricing page.

Who owns the work?

You do. Full commercial rights and source files on everything we deliver.

Can we see the work before committing?

Yes. Send a brief and we will produce a real piece on your own brand within 3 working days, at no cost and with no card.
