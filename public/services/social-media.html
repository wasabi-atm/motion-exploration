Our Efficient Reformatting Process

Transforming single-source content for multi-platform success

In today’s digital landscape, a one-size-fits-all approach to content rarely delivers results. All of our social media video projects are built to scale, making it easy to repurpose your primary video for different platforms. Our specialized process adapts each version for maximum impact across social media channels.
One video, multiple platforms, endless possibilities

Our streamlined approach

Our expert team analyzes your content and crafts tailored versions for each social media platform. If you share your brand assets, reference links, and campaign goal, we can start right away. From in-depth YouTube videos to snappy Instagram Reels, we ensure your message resonates everywhere within just 24 hours.

Source material analysis

We begin with your original content, typically a 3-minute event highlight video in 16:9 aspect ratio. Our team analyzes the key messages and visual elements to inform our reformatting strategy.

Platform-specific optimization

YouTube: We enhance the full-length video with animated typography and graphics, optimizing it for in-depth viewing and SEO benefits.

LinkedIn: A concise 1-minute version is created, featuring a targeted call-to-action tailored for professional audiences and B2B engagement.

Instagram and TikTok: We produce multiple 15-second clips in both 1:1 and 9:16 aspect ratios, perfect for Reels and short-form content. Platform-appropriate audio is added to enhance engagement and trend compatibility.

Rapid turnaround and delivery

Our efficient process ensures all reformatted content is completed within 24 hours. The finalized assets are then uploaded directly to your designated drive, organized for immediate access and utilization.

Frequently asked questions

Got questions?
We've got answers

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

Do You Support Multiple Aspect Ratios?

We can export videos in multiple aspect ratios for our clients (9:16, 1:1, 16:9, etc.) and upload these to a cloud-based drive or project management system of your choice for easy access and use by your content or social media team.

What Video Formats Do You Use?

The majority of videos are exported in MP4 unless otherwise requested by our clients.

How much does social media video production cost?

Pricing depends on how many videos you need, the formats (9:16, 1:1, 16:9), and how fast you want them delivered. Most teams choose a subscription so you get predictable monthly pricing and steady output. Share your goals and content volume, and we’ll recommend the best-fit package.

l am still not sure if this is right for me, can we discuss it first?

Of course. The fastest way to see if this is a fit is to book a quick call. You’ll walk through your goals, content volume, and platforms, and you’ll leave with a clear recommendation on scope, pricing, and timeline. If it makes sense, you can request a free sample right after the call.

Why should I create effective branded motion graphics for my social media channels?

Branded motion graphics are easy to reuse and provide consistency. They can be used across a variety of video editing software and allow you to maintain a strong visual identity even when using different editors.

How can creating motion graphics for social media benefit my business's online presence?

By having a clear identity and reusable assets, it allows you to create content at scale and have multiple people, both in-house and outsourced, creating video content that is cohesive and aligns with your brand.
