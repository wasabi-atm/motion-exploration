Frequently asked questions

Questions
scaleup companies usually ask us

We want to make sure scaleup companies feel confident that our service is right and well-suited. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

We just raised. What video should we make first?

Almost always the flagship explainer. It's the one video that works everywhere at once, on your homepage, at the start of every sales deck, and cut down for social and paid. Most teams get more from one excellent explainer than from three average videos, and everything else can build from it later.

We're bootstrapped, or between rounds. Does this still apply to us?

Yes. The funding labels are shorthand for situations, not requirements. Series A here really means one small team carrying a launch, Series B means shipping faster than your content can keep up, and Series C means keeping many videos consistent at once. Tell us which of those sounds like your week and we'll work from there, whether you've raised or not.

We only have budget for one video. How do we make it count?

By planning the versions before we make the original. One explainer becomes the homepage video, the sales deck opener, and a set of short cut-downs for social and paid, all from a single project. That's the difference between one video and one asset library.

Our brand isn't finished yet, or it keeps changing. What happens to our videos?

Both are normal at your stage, and we build for them. We can start from a pitch deck and whatever visual direction exists so far, and videos are made in modular scenes, so when your brand evolves we update colours, type, and key frames instead of starting from nothing. Primer has rebranded three times since 2023 and we've built through every version, including one project where the new guidelines were still being written.

Our product ships every few weeks. How do you keep up?

By not starting from zero each time. On a subscription we already know your brand, your product, and your standards, so a new feature video starts from a brief, not a re-introduction. The reusable asset library we build along the way makes each video faster than the one before it.

When does a subscription make more sense than fixed-price projects?

When launches start arriving faster than one-off briefs can handle. We'll tell you honestly when that point comes, because past a certain pace the subscription simply costs less. Obrizum started with a single video and grew into a two year subscription and more than a hundred videos since.

Can you handle several launches at the same time?

Yes. Subscription clients get a dedicated team rather than a single person, including your own account manager and project manager, so parallel projects don't queue behind each other and video twelve is made by the same people as video one. ClickUp ran more than 30 projects with us in six months, most of them overlapping.

How do you keep videos consistent across different products and teams?

One partner, one asset library, one standard. Every video draws on the same visual system we've built with you, so a launch video for one product line matches the onboarding video for another, even when different teams requested them months apart.

Can you localise videos for new markets?

Yes. We deliver versions in different languages and formats from the same master project, which costs far less than making each market's video from scratch. Dext's launch videos included a French edition alongside the English original, cut for every placement they needed.

Several of our teams will be requesting videos. How does that work without chaos?

Through one shared workflow instead of scattered email threads. You submit and prioritise requests through your dashboard, track progress in the credit tracker, and leave feedback directly on storyboards in Figma, so every team sees the same picture.

We already have an in-house creative team. Where do you fit?

Alongside them, not instead of them. Dext has motion capability in-house and works with us to run more launches in parallel than their own team has room for. Your team keeps the work closest to the brand, we add capacity where the volume is.

Will we outgrow you?

The companies at the far end of this range are already here. ClickUp, Mirakl, and HackerRank all work with us at a scale most scaleups are still growing toward. Whatever stage you reach, you won't be the biggest company we work with.
What if we need changes after the video is delivered?  We work with you until it's right. If something needs adjusting after delivery, tell us what's off and we'll work through the changes with you rather than pointing at a revision policy.

The subscription starts from $3,680 per month and tends to take over from Series B onward, once launches come faster than one-off briefs can.
