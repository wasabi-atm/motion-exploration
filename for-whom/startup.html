What we learned

One hire cannot cover everything a startup needs

A startup can need a launch video, pitch deck, landing page, product demo, and social content in the same month. That is more than one generalist can reasonably own, especially while the product, positioning, and brand are still changing.
A full-stack marketing hire now costs $120,000 to $180,000 a year before benefits and tools, and takes three to four months to find and ramp up (Stackmatix).
An agency can typically be onboarded in about two weeks and start producing in the first 30 days, months faster than a new hire reaches the same point (Stackmatix).
The median startup spends around 8% of revenue on marketing, which at $2 million in annual revenue doesn't even cover one fully loaded hire (SaaS Capital).
One hire brings one person’s skill set. A creative partner gives you access to animators, designers, scriptwriters, and project support as the work changes.

Frequently asked questions

Questions startup companies ask us most often

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

How fast can you deliver YC startup projects?

Speed is critical for YC startups. During the YC S25 batch, we worked on over half a dozen YC projects simultaneously, including Rimba, Nixo, Hera, Finto, and many more. Each project had the same “demo day” deadline, and we delivered every video within two weeks without sacrificing quality. Typical timelines for other projects range from 1–3 weeks depending on complexity, with options for urgent turnarounds.

How do you keep costs within a startup’s budget, especially for YC companies just starting out?

We maximize efficiency by leveraging our large asset library from past projects, adapting and tweaking existing assets to fit a startup’s branding. Our robust workflow ensures no time is wasted, making the most of every resource. For projects that allow it, we also utilize AI for voiceovers and video generation, further reducing costs while maintaining high-quality output.

What experience does Motion The Agency have working with startups?

We have extensive experience supporting startups at all stages. From early-stage YC S25 companies like Rimba, Nixo, and Finto, to VC-backed startups scaling into mid-market companies like Asset-Map, we understand the fast-moving needs, tight timelines, and resource constraints that startups face.

Can YC startups see a sample before committing

Yes, they can. In fact, Finto, one of the first YC companies we worked with last year, started with a sample project before moving into a full partnership. This allows startups to see our quality, workflow, and approach before making any commitment.

What level of creative involvement will you have?

We can handle everything from A–Z in motion design and web design, but our involvement depends on what the company needs. Since startup teams are usually busy developing products, we often take care of the full process — from scripting to the finished video — ensuring the final output aligns perfectly with product messaging and launch goals.

Can Motion The Agency help with startup pitch videos for investors?

Yes — Motion The Agency creates investor pitch videos that clearly communicate a startup’s vision, product, and market opportunity. We helped TaskNet produce a video used as their pitch opener and worked with Luniate on both the pitch video and refining their deck for maximum impact.

How much does a startup explainer video cost?

Pricing starts at $3,480 for a 30-second Basic animation, with Premium and Enhanced packages for more complex videos. All packages include a free video sample, and you can check our pricing page.
