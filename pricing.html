Frequently asked questions

Got questions?
‍We've got answers

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

Which model is right for us?

One video or a handful a year, fixed price is better value. Video every month, a subscription costs less past roughly four videos a year. Credits sit between the two. Send us a brief and we will tell you which fits, including when the answer is the cheaper one.

What does each model include?

The full process, from planning and storyboarding through to final delivery in the formats you need. Fixed price scopes that to one project. Subscription gives you a named team every month. Credits give you a prepaid balance to spend as work comes up.

Can we switch models later?

Yes. Most clients start with one project and move to a subscription once they need output monthly. We work out the details with you on a call.

What drives the price?

Length, animation style and how much is built from scratch. A 60 second 2D explainer using your brand assets sits at the lower end. 3D, custom illustration and sound design move it up.

How fast is delivery?

Six working days for a 60 second video, eight for 90 seconds. Faster is available at additional cost when a launch date will not move.

What are the payment terms?

Everything upfront. Fixed price and credits are invoiced before work starts. Subscriptions run on the calendar month.

Who owns the finished work?

You do. Full commercial rights and source files on everything we deliver.

Is the free sample really free?

Yes. Real work on your own brand in 3 working days, no card and nothing to sign. If it is not right, you have lost nothing.
