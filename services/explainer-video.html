The definition

What are explainer videos?

Explainer videos simplify complex ideas, products, or services into short, engaging animations or videos. They’re your ultimate tool for capturing attention, building trust, and driving action. Whether you’re launching a new product, onboarding users, or pitching to investors, explainer videos help your audience understand your message quickly and effectively.

Explainer videos are a versatile marketing tool that captures attention and conveys messages effectively. They enhance website landing pages by engaging visitors and boosting exploration. On social media, they drive engagement with shareable content. For sales presentations and email campaigns, they add professionalism and increase conversions. Explainer videos also simplify complex ideas, making them ideal for investor pitches and other key communications.

Frequently asked questions

Got questions?
‍We've got answers

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

What’s included in your explainer video pricing?

Our pricing is fixed and transparent, with three tiers to match your needs:
- Basic: Includes 2 animation revisions
- Premium: Includes storyboard planning with 7 rounds of revisions (animations included).
- Enhanced: Includes unlimited storyboard revisions and 3D animation.
- Voiceover (VO): Professional voiceover is available at an additional cost.
‍
Costs scale based on video length (e.g., 30s vs. 90s) and style (2D, 3D, live-action). Get a free sample to see our quality first

How long does production take?

Most explainer video projects take 1-3 weeks from start to finish. However, you’ll receive a free storyboard or video sample within 1-3 business days to approve our direction before full production begins. For urgent projects, we offer rush options to expedite delivery.

Can I collaborate with your team during production?

Absolutely! We use a dedicated Slack channel for real-time communication and an intuitive dashboard to track progress, review notes, and approve milestones. We also make sure to communicate with you every step of the way, especially when revisions are needed, and we'll ensure that each step is approved before moving forward. You can even timestamp feedback on drafts for precision.

Do you handle multi-language or global audience needs?

Yes! We offer services of voice over in 6+ languages and video reformatting for international platforms. We work with 40+ US/UK voice artists and 20+ global artists to ensure your message resonates worldwide.

What if I need to use the video on multiple platforms?

All plans include commercial rights, and we can offer reformatting video (e.g., resizing for Instagram Reels, YouTube, or LinkedIn) based on your platform but will incur additional cost as outlined in our pricing page. We optimize aspect ratios, resolutions, and even create platform-specific edits.
