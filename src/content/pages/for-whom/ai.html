Frequently asked questions

Questions AI companies
ask us most often

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

What is an AI product video?

It's a short video built to explain what your AI product actually does and why it matters, without leaning on hype the way a lot of AI marketing does right now. Most AI companies use one to bring a skeptical audience up to speed quickly, whether that's a customer, an investor, or a partner.

How do you explain what our AI does to people who aren't technical?

We start by getting a plain language version of your product from someone on your team who understands it deeply, then build the visuals around that explanation rather than around technical language. The goal is a video a non technical buyer can follow on the first watch, without losing anything that matters to a technical one.

Can you make a video without exposing exactly how our model works?

Yes. Most AI companies we work with want to show what their product does without revealing the mechanics behind it, and that's normal. We build the visuals around outcomes and behaviour rather than internal architecture, and we're happy to put an NDA in place before any technical discussion if that would help your team feel comfortable.

Our product changes every few weeks. How do you stop the video going out of date?

We build videos in modular scenes wherever possible, so if a specific feature changes, we can update that section without reanimating the whole video. We'll flag this during discovery if your product is likely to move fast, so we can plan for it from the start.

We don't have a finished product yet, only a working prototype. Can you still make something?

Yes, this comes up often. We can build a video around your product vision and roadmap rather than the current build, which is exactly what we did for Kuberno ahead of their funding round. We'll just need close input from your team to keep it accurate.

Can this double as content for investors, not just customers?

Yes. A lot of our AI clients use the same video, or a version of it, in investor meetings and demo days as well as on their website. Tell us upfront if that's part of the plan, since it can shape how much detail we put in the opening scene.

What if we need changes after the video is delivered?

We work with you until it's right. If something needs adjusting after delivery, tell us what's off and we'll work through the changes with you rather than pointing at a revision policy.

Can we use the video across different platforms?

Yes. We deliver your final video in the formats each channel needs, so the same project works on your website, social channels, and anywhere else you plan to use it.

What's the pricing structure, and how do I get started?

We offer a fixed price per video with a clear quote upfront, or a monthly subscription if you're likely to need ongoing content. Book a call or request a free sample and we'll ask a few questions about your product before quoting.
