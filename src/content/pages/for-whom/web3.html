What we learned

Getting people interested is only the first step

People may be curious about Web3, but curiosity alone does not turn them into users. They still need to understand what the product does, what they gain from using it, and whether they feel confident enough to take part.
Many people who have never owned cryptocurrency say they still do not understand how it works. When the basics feel unclear, even a useful product can lose people before its value comes through. (The Motley Fool)
A large share of adults remain unsure whether cryptocurrency is safe or reliable. Web3 products need to give new users enough clarity to make their own informed decision. (Pew Research Center)
Crypto scams and fraud took an estimated $17 billion in 2025. Every legitimate Web3 company is entering a market where new users are already more careful about what they support. (Chainalysis)
Many Web3 losses come from access-control failures rather than the underlying technology. Users need to understand how a platform approaches security, ownership, and accountability. (Hacken)

Frequently asked questions

Questions Web3 companies
usually ask us

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

What is Web3 video production?

It's video content built to explain a blockchain, crypto, or decentralized project clearly, while also addressing the skepticism a lot of Web3 audiences start with. Some clients search for this as a crypto explainer video, it's the same thing.

Our space has a bad reputation because of scams. How do you help with that, not just the explanation?

We lean on specifics rather than hype, real team members, real partners, real mechanics, rather than the kind of vague, exciting language that tends to read as a red flag to a skeptical audience. Showing your project clearly is itself part of building trust.

Our audience is mostly newcomers who don't understand blockchain at all. How technical should the video be?

Usually not very. We build for someone with zero prior knowledge first, then let anyone more experienced pick up the extra detail from context. Over-explaining thing they don't conclusively understand yet tends to lose newcomers faster than under-explaining it.

Can you help with a token launch video specifically?

Yes. These usually need to do two jobs at once, explain the tokenomics clearly and build genuine excitement, without tipping into the kind of hype language that makes people skeptical. We can talk through the balance during discovery.

Do you understand the difference between our various Web3 products, like an NFT app versus a gaming platform?

We treat each product as its own explanation rather than reusing the same script with different logos. This was the case for Venly, where we built three genuinely different videos for three genuinely different products under one engagement.

What if we need changes after the video is delivered?

We work with you until it's right. If something needs adjusting after delivery, tell us what's off and we'll work through the changes with you rather than pointing at a revision policy.

Can we use the video across different platforms?

Yes. We deliver your final video in the formats each channel needs, so the same project works on your website, social channels, and anywhere else you plan to use it.

What's the pricing structure, and how do I get started?

We offer a fixed price per video with a clear quote upfront, or a monthly subscription if you're likely to need ongoing content. Book a call or request a free sample and we'll ask a few questions about your project before quoting.
