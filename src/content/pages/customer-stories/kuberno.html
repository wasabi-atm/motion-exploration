THE CLIENT

Kuberno

You may have heard of Kuberno, a leader in the legal and RegTech space. They work with major clients like Deliveroo, Citizens, and Nationwide, providing solutions for entity management and compliance. In October 2024, they raised $9.5 million in Series A funding, backed by Barclays and Nasdaq, positioning them for rapid growth. As part of their marketing strategy, Kuberno wanted to use videos to showcase their product vision and future plans, helping their audience understand the direction of their platform and the impact they aim to deliver. With ambitious goals in place, they were ready to elevate their brand and communicate their strategy effectively.

THE Timeline

How we did it

Creating a product launch video for an evolving product that communicates Kuberno’s future plans and vision under a tight deadline isn’t easy. We’ve developed a robust workflow that we adapt to meet our clients’ needs, this is how we did it:
Hyperexponential:  more storytelling-led, focused on narrative and momentum.
Inbenta: more abstract, using visuals to communicate ideas.
Adaptify Meta:  more product and feature-focused, useful if you want to highlight functionality.

Aligning expectations from the get go

5 x References sent to the client for feedback.
After our initial email exchange, before the first meeting, we sent over video references and a storyboard example. This helped us align quickly on the client’s general ideas. From these references, we got a solid understanding of their feedback on key elements like:
Speed
Duration
Voiceover
Communication style
Transitions
Color palette
Audio levels
Use of brand assets

Multiple samples

2 alternative versions, 10-second video sample
To help us know what exactly Kuberno want in their video, we sent out 2 different 10 seconds video samples, with 2 completely different styles. This also helps give us a better understanding on their outlook for their brand and how it’s evolving.

Aligning with Kuberno's rebranding in 24 hours

3 style frames, using Kuberno’s color palette and chosen style
After Kuberno chose the sample, we moved quickly to align with their rebranding. Within 24 hours, we delivered style frames in PNG format based on their website’s brand colors and the chosen style. For example, product shots featured a low-transparency gradient fade-off, maintaining their current branding while ensuring flexibility for the rebrand.

Tight feedback loop

Feedback and revisions completed within 1 working day.
Storyboard finalized in 5 days, with 24-hour revision turnaround after feedback. The changes ranged from script adjustments to visual tweaks and even animation movements, ensuring everything aligned with Kuberno's evolving style.
Download Storyboard

Parallel work

2-3 people worked on the project at all times.
This project combined both 3D and 2D animation to create a flexible video that could adapt to Kuberno’s evolving branding. To achieve this, we broke the project into scenes and assigned them to multiple animators, ensuring each part matched the desired style.
