Innovative Learning Solutions

Why choose animated  
video for training

Animated videos transform complex information into engaging, memorable content. They boost retention, simplify updates, and provide measurable results. Choose animation to make your training more effective, flexible, and impactful.

Efficiency

Beyond creation, we offer tools to track engagement and assess comprehension. Understand the impact of your training videos with actionable insights, ensuring continuous improvement and a robust return on your educational investment.

Clarity

Animation allows us to explain complex concepts with precision. We can break down intricate topics and make them easily understandable.

Flexibility

Unlike recorded videos, animated content allows for seamless updates. Keep your training courses up-to-date without breaking the bank.

Return on Investment

Sustainable training solution investment

Training is essential for any business, ensuring that staff and processes are efficient. The initial investment in building a video training course provides long-term returns, offering a cost-effective solution that can enhance productivity and remain relevant for many years.

Increased employee engagement

Employees value opportunities for growth, and research shows that access to engaging e-learning increases both productivity and satisfaction. Offering interactive video training not only boosts engagement but also improves retention rates, creating a more motivated and committed workforce.

Reduced training time

Animated video training provides two key benefits: it is more cost-effective than traditional videography, and it’s easier to update. Course content can be remotely revised without needing to rehire videographers, resulting in lower training costs and less time required to implement updates.

Enhanced compliance

The training needs of each industry vary, but animated video training is frequently used for data protection, cybersecurity, and workplace health and safety. This method helps ensure compliance, reducing the risk of regulatory breaches while providing an engaging and effective training experience.

Improved learning outcomes

Video is a vital part of effective learning, but its impact is amplified when used alongside Learning Management Systems (LMS) or Virtual Learning Environments (VLE). This combination leads to better learning outcomes and stronger knowledge retention, resulting in more effective training experiences.

Scalable training solutions

Animated corporate training enables easy content updates and additions to existing modules, providing a scalable solution for growing businesses. This approach allows organizations to continuously enhance their training programs while maintaining alignment with their brand and adapting to changing training needs.

Frequently asked questions

Got questions?
We've got answers

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

What types of training videos do you produce?

We produce a wide range of corporate training videos, including:

  • Onboarding and orientation videos
  • Compliance training videos
  • Product and service knowledge videos
  • Technical skills training videos
  • Cybersecurity awareness videos
  • Health and safety training videos

Our animated videos are tailored to your specific needs and designed to engage and educate your workforce effectively.

How long does it take to produce a training video?

The production time can vary depending on the complexity and length of the video. We follow a structured process that includes content collection, creative alignment, and sample creation before moving into full production. For a more accurate timeline, we recommend discussing your specific project requirements with our team.

Can you update existing training videos?

Yes, we offer flexible solutions for updating existing training videos. Unlike traditional videography, our animated content allows for seamless updates. This makes it easy to keep your training courses up-to-date without significant costs, ensuring your content remains relevant and accurate.

Do you offer voice-over services in multiple languages?

While not explicitly mentioned in the provided content, it's common for professional video production services to offer multi-language voice-over options. We recommend contacting our team directly to discuss specific language requirements for your project.

Can you integrate the videos with our existing Learning Management System (LMS)?

Yes, our training videos are designed to work seamlessly with Learning Management Systems (LMS) or Virtual Learning Environments (VLE). This integration enhances learning outcomes and knowledge retention, creating more effective training experiences for your team.

What file formats do you deliver the final videos in?

We deliver our videos in industry-standard formats that are compatible with most platforms and devices. For specific format requirements, please discuss your needs with our team during the project planning phase.

Do you offer any analytics or tracking features with the videos?

Yes, we provide tools to track engagement and assess comprehension. These analytics offer actionable insights into the impact of your training videos, allowing for continuous improvement and helping you measure the return on your educational investment.

How do you handle confidential or sensitive information in training videos?

We take the security and confidentiality of your information very seriously. Our process includes:

  • Strict confidentiality measures
  • Limited access controls
  • IP protection
  • Clear ownership agreements
  • Secure file transfer
  • Data destruction options
  • Non-disclosure agreements for all team members

We ensure that your sensitive content is safeguarded throughout the entire production process.

Can we reuse the animated characters or elements in future videos?

Yes, one of the benefits of choosing animated videos for training is the ability to reuse and repurpose elements. This approach allows for scalable training solutions, making it easy to update content or add to existing modules as your business grows and training needs evolve. It also helps maintain consistency in your brand and visual style across multiple training videos.
