Vision

The Planful story: 1 forward-thinking company. 4 unique brands. 100+ creative assets delivered.

Planful is a financial performance management company that helps finance and accounting teams plan, close, and report with speed and accuracy. As a forward-thinking enterprise, they manage four distinct brands: Planful, Perform25, AI Labs, and Meet the CFO. Each brand has its own style and audience, so our focus was on creating a video marketing plan for each one that could stand strong on its own while still feeling part of the bigger Planful story. Together, we turned complex design ideas into clear, consistent, and engaging content that built real impact.

What was included in Planful’s 12-month creative subscription?

Just like any of our subscription projects, we built a dedicated team around Planful’s needs. Our support covered everything from ideation and storyboarding to script writing and full content production — all the way through to ready-to-publish video marketing assets.

What types of video assets were created in the Planful project?

We produced a variety of marketing videos across Planful’s brands. This included launch trailers for the Perform25 event and AI Labs rollout, short podcast clips for social media, and a brand explainer video for Planful. Beyond publish-ready videos, we also created custom MOGRTs and lower-thirds to keep their internal team’s future edits consistent and on-brand.

How many video and motion graphic assets were produced during the 12-month subscription?

We created over 100 animation assets for Planful, covering everything from event trailers and explainer videos to social media content and animated countdowns. Each piece was designed to align with their video marketing plan and support different stages of the user journey.
