Dove Hero: Make marketing simple and accessible for your real estate business

DoveHero is the all-in-one marketing tool built to help businesses grow
by making campaigns easier and more efficient. As one of our
subscription clients, DoveHero has a user-friendly platform that brings
together email marketing, SMS, and WhatsApp campaigns, letting you reach
thousands of contacts without the hassle. It’s all about saving time,
working smarter, and driving real results without the extra stress.
This wasn’t just about a fresh logo; it was about crafting a brand and digital presence that matched their ambition, stood out in the competitive AI space, and resonated with their target audience.
Through close collaboration and iterative feedback, we partnered with them to launch their new identity and create a powerful foundation for growth, ensuring their brand reflected their evolution and their goals as a startup.
Project type
Subscription
Project Cost
$19,000
Project timeline
 12 months
Project Scope

Project overview

DoveHero, as a marketing tool, needed a dynamic and interactive website to showcase its brand identity. The challenge was to strike the right balance between providing clear information about DoveHero’s services while keeping visitors engaged with visually compelling and interactive elements, including an explainer video, Lottie animation, and website design.

Brand identity

As we worked with DoveHero over time, we saw them grow into a brand that needed to stand out in a crowded market. We helped them create a bold, distinct presence that not only sets them apart but also connects with their values and the audience they want to reach.

Website design

We brought DoveHero’s dream website to life, building it from the ground up to ensure a seamless experience. Every detail was designed with their audience in mind, making navigation effortless while helping visitors learn more about DoveHero and build trust in the brand.

Lottie animation

To make their message clear and help users navigate their UI more smoothly, DoveHero teamed up with us to add Lottie animations to their website. This made it easier to explain their ideas while keeping the experience fun, engaging, and easy to follow.

Explainer  videos

DoveHero came to us looking for a way to communicate their big ideas and showcase their product clearly. We helped them bring it to life with an engaging explainer video that breaks everything down in a way that’s easy to understand and visually compelling.
