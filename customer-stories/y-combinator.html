Workflow

How we managed tight

deadlines without burning out

Every project has different requirements. Some require more time for storyboarding, while others need extra time for animation. When several YC clients approached us with the same Demo Day deadline, we reorganised our workflow and allocated additional resources to ensure that each project remained on track and that we delivered high-quality results on time.

Planning the workflow

Our project managers mapped out each task, assigning the right mix of designers, storyboard artists, and animators. This made priorities clear and kept every project on track.

Allocating resources

For YC projects with tight timelines, we brought in extra hands and extended hours. That helped us deliver quickly while keeping quality and detail consistent.

Keeping things moving

While one group focused on YC clients, others handled ongoing projects. This system kept everything balanced deadlines met, teams supported, and all clients satisfied.
