Frequently asked questions

Got Questions?
We've got answers

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

What is the difference between Basic, Premium and Enhanced?

Basic is simple 2D animation with no storyboard, built for social content and marketing collateral. Premium adds storyboard planning, 2.5D and basic sound design, which suits explainer videos and product demos. Enhanced adds 3D and enhanced sound design for work that needs to look its best.

What does a video cost?

Price is set by length and package, and every combination is in the table above. A 60 second video is $4,480 on Basic, $5,140 on Premium and $5,930 on Enhanced. You see the number before you commit, and it does not change afterwards.

How fast will we get it?

A 60 second Basic video takes 6 working days and a 90 second takes 8. Premium and Enhanced take longer because storyboard planning happens before anyone animates. Every timeline sits next to its price in the table.

Can you go faster than that?

Yes. Super fast delivery puts double the team on your project for 30 percent more, which roughly halves the timeline. A 60 second Basic video drops from 6 working days to 3.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of animation revisions on every package. Premium and Enhanced also include storyboard revisions, which is where most changes actually happen. Catching direction at the storyboard stage is faster and cheaper than reworking finished animation.

What do you need from us to start?

A rough idea of what the video needs to do, plus your brand assets if you have them. There is no lengthy creative brief to fill in. If you have a script we will work from it, and if you do not we will write one with you.

What can we add on?

Professional voiceover with full commercial rights in over six languages. Reformatting to 9:16 and 1:1 for social platforms. Duration adjustment to cut shorter versions from the same video. Each is priced separately and quoted before you approve.

What if we need more than one video?

Tell us and we will price it properly rather than selling you the same thing repeatedly. Past roughly four videos a year, a subscription or Creative Credits costs less for identical scope, and we will say so.

What is your refund policy?

We have a 14 day money back policy. If you are not happy with the work, you can cancel and we refund you in full.
