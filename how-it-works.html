Frequently asked questions

Got questions? we've got answers

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

What is included in your video service?

We specialize in 2D and 3D animation, branded motion graphics, social media motion graphics, product video, and UI animations. We will create a custom video to fit your needs! We work with advertising agencies and marketers or directly with companies and individuals.

We have existing video footage. Do you still charge per minute?

We won’t charge you for the duration of the video. We will charge you for the duration of the animation we add to your video. If you send a 3 minute video clip to us and we create and add a 15 second introduction, you will only be charged for 15 seconds (1/4 minute) of animation.

How do I know which package suits my needs?

Perhaps your needs are simple, and you only need a few videos each month. Maybe you’re skyrocketing with your business and need lots of videos at a great price. In either case, we have the package for you to help bring your brand to life. Check our pricing page or feel free to contact us for more information.

How fast are your turnaround times like?

We aim to deliver completely edited and annotated videos in 2 business days. Although we try to deliver everything as quickly as possible, larger time-intensive projects may take more than 2-3 days to complete and we’ll set realistic expectations for you as the requests come in.
