Frequently asked questions

Got questions?
‍We've got answers

We want to make sure you feel confident that our service is right for you. Here we’ve looked to address some commonly asked questions. If you have any more specific questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with a member of the team.

What are your working hour for US clients

We are a global team with flexible working hours, allowing us to ensure seamless communication and collaboration with our US-based clients, no matter the time zone.

Do you understand the niche trends for US-based businesses?

Yes, 70% of our clientele is US-based, and we frequently partner with Y Combinator companies in San Francisco. This gives us a deep understanding of the trends and needs specific to US businesses, especially in the tech, AI and B2B

How long does production take?

A standard explainer video runs 2–4 weeks from kickoff to delivery. Rush timelines are available depending on scope. Our async collaboration model means you're never waiting around for a meeting  feedback happens on your schedule, not ours, using Loom and Frame.io for asynchronous reviews.

How many revisions do we get?

We build revision rounds into every project stage  script, storyboard, animation, and final. You'll never hit a wall mid project. Our goal is to get it right, not to run out the clock on your revision credits. Scope changes are discussed openly, upfront.

Can I see a sample before committing?

es — and it's genuinely free, not a bait-and-switch. Before any contract or payment, we'll create a custom storyboard or short video sample tailored to your product. It's how we prove the fit, not just describe it

What does the onboarding process look like?

It starts with a 30-min discovery call (or async brief if you prefer). From there: kickoff → script → storyboard approval → animation → final delivery. Every step has a clear owner, a timeline, and a feedback window. No back-and-forth chaos, no missing deliverables.
